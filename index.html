VAlliance Ambush - Operational Playbook

New alliance intel added without removing the existing account strategy.

Latest safe launch = 3:00 + your march time. Every rally has a 3-minute preparation timer. If the hit lands after the event ends, it deals zero damage.
  1. Fill R4/R5 bonus rallies first

    Prioritize alliance-leader bonus rallies while there is enough time. Fill them quickly instead of letting several rallies sit half-empty.

  2. Relaunch immediately

    As soon as a march returns, resupply and get back into a rally. Ambush rewards repeated hits, not one large hit followed by idle time.

  3. Stay close to the Assembly Point

    Shorter march time means more completed hits and a later final safe-launch cutoff. Teleport closer when practical.

  4. Start at least 5 rallies and join at least 5

    Alliance guide personal quest checklist targets; then continue pushing total damage.
